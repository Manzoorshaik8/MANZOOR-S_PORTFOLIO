Who I Am

Crafting cinematic stories,
one frame at a time

I'm Manzoor Shaik,(M.C.A Graduate.Bengaluru) founder of Key Frames Media — a photographer, cinematographer,Worked As AD and video editor built around one belief: every frame should earn its place in the story.

What began as a personal fascination with light and motion has grown into a full-service studio covering weddings, corporate brands, products and automobiles across Andhra Pradesh. My approach blends technical precision — composition, color, sound — with a documentary instinct for genuine, unscripted moments.

Mission: to give every client a film and a gallery that feels like them, not a template. Vision: to build Key Frames Media into the region's most trusted name in visual storytelling.
